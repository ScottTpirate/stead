# Work Graph contract

The Work Graph is a rebuildable projection of OWGP typed relationships, not a new ontology or system of record. It connects Work, Docs, Teams, People, Agents, and applicable Code/Delivery resources. Edge direction is fixed by DOM-006; edge visibility is at least the joined restriction of endpoint and relationship metadata. The query and rebuild contract is [search-resource-model.md](./search-resource-model.md).
